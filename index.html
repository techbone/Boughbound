A small game
about possibility.

You guide every living tip with the same motion. Pale light adds points, golden fork buds grow a new branch, and thorns prune whatever touches them. More living tips multiply the light you gather.

Reach the canopy in 90 seconds. Keep at least one tip alive. Your finished portrait preserves your branching paths in a compressed botanical drawing.
